We do not buy stock graphics. We do not print generic merchandise. We partner with digital saboteurs, noise musicians, typographic vandals, and underground creators to build high-contrast streetwear.

THE COLLAB PROTOCOLS

01

HIGH CONTRAST / TWO TONE MAXIMUM

Our heavy industrial screen press runs best with raw separation. Submissions must be pure vector outlines or extreme halftone patterns. No gradients, no soft blurs, and absolutely no watercolor effects.

02

AGGRESSIVE PLACEMENT OPTIONS

We print over seams, wrapped around ribs, wrapped down the collar, or oversized across the shoulder blades. Design for the entire physical structure of a 380GSM heavy box fit tee.

03

RAW INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

You retain ownership of your graphic work. We run a 50/50 net profit split on every unit sold during the drop window. No hidden licensing traps, just direct street distribution.

THE LAB MANIFESTO

WE PRINT
THE EXTREME.

The street is flooded with identical digital print-on-demand designs that disintegrate after three washes. Our lab operates differently. We pull thick, toxic-colored plastisol inks through heavy wood-framed silk screens.

The result is a heavy, tactile print with distinct raised textures that breaks in beautifully over years of wear. If your design isn't ready to survive a mosh pit, it doesn't belong in our catalog.
